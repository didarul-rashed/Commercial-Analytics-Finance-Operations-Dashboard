## 🔎 Project Overview
This project is based on a **real-world business problem** I solved within the **Commercial Department**.

The goal was to build a **centralized Commercial Analytics Dashboard** in Power BI by integrating multiple commercial functions including **Bill Settlement, Fund Realization, MOB, Master Order Management, PI, and LC Operations**.

For confidentiality, confidential business information, operational details, and financial figures have been intentionally blurred or modified in the screenshots. However, the dashboard architecture, analytical approach, business rules, and reporting logic accurately reflect the actual project delivered.

👉 This demonstrates the business solution while respecting organizational data privacy.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
The commercial team faced challenges with:

- **Tracking bill settlement and outstanding liabilities**
- **Monitoring export fund realization performance**
- **Analyzing MOB surplus / deficit positions**
- **Managing PI and LC operations through separate reports**
- **Monitoring customer order revisions and delivery pushbacks**
- **Limited visibility across commercial functions**
- **Manual reporting and delayed decision-making**

---

## 🚀 Solution Delivered

### 1. **Bill Settlement Analytics**
- Outstanding and settled bill monitoring
- Due vs. overdue exposure tracking
- Aging analysis and settlement KPIs
- Bank-wise and plant-wise performance monitoring

### 2. **Fund Realization Analytics**
- Export realization monitoring
- RF vs. Non-RF analysis
- Realization cycle tracking
- Factory and buyer performance insights

### 3. **MOB Analytics**
- Monthly surplus / deficit monitoring
- Opening vs. closing balance tracking
- Budget transfer and recovery analysis
- Plant-wise and bank-wise MOB performance

### 4. **Master Order Analytics**
- Initial vs. revised CBD monitoring
- Customer PO analysis
- Order pushback tracking
- Order execution and planning insights

### 5. **PI & LC Analytics**
- PI issuance monitoring
- LC tracking and utilization analysis
- Commercial workflow visibility
- Bank-wise exposure analysis

---

## 💡 Business Impact
- ✅ **Centralized visibility** across commercial operations
- ✅ Improved **cash flow and realization monitoring**
- ✅ Faster identification of outstanding and delayed transactions
- ✅ Enhanced **MOB performance tracking**
- ✅ Better visibility into order planning and execution risks

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Power BI**: Data modeling, DAX measures, Power Query
- **Excel / SharePoint**: Data sources
- **Advanced DAX**: Financial and operational KPI calculations

## 🗂️ Data Model

A centralized commercial data model was developed to connect order management, commercial finance, banking, and realization processes into a single analytical framework.

This model enables seamless drill-down analysis across multiple business functions while ensuring consistent KPI calculations throughout the dashboard.
